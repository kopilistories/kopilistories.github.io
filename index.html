কালদিয়া

34 min • Assamese with English subtitles

Kaldiya is a contemplative Assamese science drama that follows a physics student whose growing fascination with detecting earthquakes slowly leads him towards an ancient mystery hidden in the lower Brahmaputra valley of Assam. Seen through the evolving relationship between an inquisitive student and the teacher who worries deeply for his future, the film explores curiosity, memory, science and the quiet wonder that often lies hidden within ordinary lives. Although rooted in the landscapes, folklore and history of Assam, Kaldiya is ultimately a human story... one where forgotten places, scientific inquiry and lived experience gradually converge without offering easy answers. The film is the opening chapter of the Kachpur Verse, a planned trilogy exploring memory, history, identity and humanity's enduring desire to understand the unknown.

About

KOPILI STORIES is an independent film studio based in Assam, India. Our films explore memory, place, science and human relationships through stories rooted in the landscapes and cultures of Northeast India. Rather than being defined by genre, we are interested in quiet, thoughtful cinema that finds the extraordinary within ordinary lives. We believe every place holds a memory. Every person has a story.
